// 03. PHILOSOPHY

VISION & REASON

I started building tools for businesses that couldn't afford a dev team. Over time I understood the problem wasn't design or code, but the lack of a clear and honest structure.

That's why I work with my own code, without templates or unnecessary layers. I want every business owner to understand what is under their website and have real control.

// 04. FIREWALL

WHAT I DO DIFFERENTLY?

Most freelancers leave security for the end. I put it first, from the first line of clean code.

  • I validate everything on the server. I never trust what the browser sends (real protection).
  • I use strict headers (CSP, HSTS) to block common attacks like XSS.
  • I protect every form against CSRF.
  • I keep the architecture clean and without external dependencies that could become a risk.
